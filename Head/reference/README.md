# Reference photos: real UH-1 dimensions

These images are the sources for the real UH-1 head's dimensions used in this project. They come from the ED Forums thread [Huey Collective Measurements](https://forum.dcs.world/topic/152333-huey-collective-measurements/) (Home Cockpits). Thanks to both posters for measuring and sharing them.

The images belong to their authors. They're included here, with credit, as references for the design. They are not covered by this repository's licence. The only change is that their metadata (EXIF) was stripped.

## Kornskydiver (`kornskydiver/`)

Photos posted by **Kornskydiver** on 12 January 2017 ("Here are some pictures I took a few months ago"). They show digital calipers on a real UH-1's collective head and throttle:

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `IMG_8529.jpg` | The upper box's height, calipers held upright beside it |
| `IMG_8531.jpg` | The lower box's right-hand side, calipers upright along it |
| `IMG_8532.jpg` | The throttle grip's diameter: 39.84 mm |
| `IMG_8533.jpg` | Calipers across the front of the box, over the panels |
| `IMG_8534.jpg` | Calipers across the lower panel, over the search hat |
| `IMG_8536.jpg` | The ENGINE IDLE ring round the STOP REL button: about 25.7 mm |
| `IMG_8540.jpg` | The box's depth, from the side: about 53 mm |

Readings are given only where the calipers' display is legible in the photo.

Their layout, labels and switch placement are what the Rev 5 panels follow. That includes the labels round each switch, the hat between the two lower toggles, and STOP REL beside the ENGINE IDLE button.

## dcval (`dcval/`)

Images posted by **dcval** on 27 February 2025. dcval scaled a page of the UH-1 manual from the throttle's 39.84 mm diameter (Kornskydiver's measurement) and drew the panels in Blender from it:

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `uh1-manual-page.png` | The manual page with the collective head and the panels |
| `uh1-panel-from-manual.png` | The two panels, cropped from that page |
| `blender-mesh.png` | dcval's Blender model of the panels, with dimensions: the lower panel 85 mm wide, 69.01 mm tall on the right and 47.03 mm on the left, its bottom edge 13 mm flat, 51.8 mm diagonal and 25 mm flat; the upper panel 55 × 41 mm, 15 mm in from each side |

[Head Rev 5](../Rev5/README.md) is cut to these dimensions.
