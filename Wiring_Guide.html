Overview

The HeliSR board sits upright in the head's upper box. Two 10-pin looms carry the switches, two AX ports carry the sensors, and the USB cable comes up the tube from the base. Every switch input is pulled up to 5 V on the board, so each switch only joins its input to GND.

SearchHat Rev 1.0

The stick switch is soldered to the board, so the board is the whole hat. Use either J1, with half of a 6-pin JST SR jumper (A06SR06SR30K203A), or solder wires straight to the J2 pads. J1 and J2 carry the same signals in a different order.

AS5600 sensor boards

Two identical boards: one in the base's lever (on AX1), one in the head's pod (on AX4). Each plugs in with a 1:N JST SR jumper, A04SR04SR30K152B. J1's pins are the AX port's order turned end for end, so through a 1:N jumper every signal lands on its own pin.

HeliSR AX pinSensor J1 pin
1 SDA→4 SDA
2 GND→3 GND
3 VCC→2 VCC
4 SCL→1 SCL
Never a 1:1 jumper. A part number ending in A is 1:1 and puts 5 V on the sensor's ground. Only the …152B (1:N) goes on the sensors.

Finding a toggle's lugs with a meter

  1. Set the meter to continuity. Put one probe on the centre lug.
  2. Push the lever to one position, for example INCR on the GOV switch. Touch the two outer lugs: the one that beeps is that position's lug. Mark it.
  3. Repeat for the other position. In the middle (OFF) neither should beep.
  4. Solder the centre lug to the GND chain and each marked lug to its input.

If a switch still reads the wrong way round once it's in, swap its two outer wires or swap the two inputs in the profile.

J1 loom: the toggles and STOP REL (IN1–IN8)

Half of a 10-pin JST SR jumper, plugged into J1. The wires may all be one colour: before you cut the jumper, mark the pin 1 edge, then confirm each wire with a meter against the plug's pins. Run the lower panel's GND chain from pin 1 and the upper panel's from pin 10.

Sensor leads: AX4 and AX1

Throttle: AX4 (J14) to the head's sensor

One whole 1:N jumper, no cutting. The sensor board's J1 faces up out of the pod; plug the jumper in from above once the board is in its grooves, and the other end into AX4.

Collective: AX1 (J11) to the base's sensor

Too long for one jumper: cut a 1:N jumper in half and splice the 1 m 4-wire ribbon (Adafruit 3889) between the halves. Join each wire to the wire in the same position on both sides, so the lead stays 1:N end to end.

  1. Before cutting, mark one edge wire of the jumper with a pen on both sides of where you'll cut. Mark one edge of the ribbon the same way.
  2. Cut the jumper in the middle. Strip and tin all eight ends and the ribbon's.
  3. At each end, join marked to marked, then the rest in order. Heat-shrink each joint on its own, then the bundle.
  4. Feed the sensor end down the tube and through the lever's axial bore before closing the lever halves; plug it into the sensor board's J1 in the pocket.

USB cable

Before you power it

  1. Sensor boards: with nothing plugged in, VCC to GND (J1 pin 2 to pin 3) is not a short, and U1's pins 1 and 2 aren't joined.
  2. Each sensor lead: AX-end pin 1 to sensor-end pin 4, 2 to 3, 3 to 2, 4 to 1, and no two wires joined.
  3. Each loom: with every switch released or OFF, no input pin reads continuity to GND (pins 1 and 10). Then work each switch: only its own input joins GND.
  4. Hat: tilt each way and press the middle; each joins its own input to GND, a diagonal joins two.
  5. Then plug in the USB and use HeliSR Configurator's input page: every switch should light its own input, and the AS5600 helper should find a sensor on AX1 and AX4.
